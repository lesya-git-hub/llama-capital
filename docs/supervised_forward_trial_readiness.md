## Proposed daily operating rule — design only

1. Fix one strategy variant, 90-day start/end, six-symbol universe, EUR 10,000 strict ledger, existing risk limits, explicit costs, FX age policy and end-of-trial valuation rule before rehearsal. Obtain 200 aligned historical sessions for warm-up under permitted rights; five days of connectivity data are insufficient. Do not mix variants in one ledger.
2. For session Monday, wait until the authoritative session has closed **and** required publication/finality evidence is available. A regular scheduled close may be 16:00 New York; use the actual calendar for early/exceptional closes. Record effective session, publication, acquisition and decision times separately in UTC, with New York session labels. Next-morning acquisition is a convenience, never finality proof. If evidence is unavailable, stop before signal generation through the existing completed-candle contracts.
3. Validate the full batch and history, then present an operator review. Missing/stale/duplicate bars, revisions, missing action evidence, calendar conflicts or absent FX cause a HOLD of the decision, not forward-fill, fallback or reconstructed evidence. Never retroactively overwrite an earlier decision or performance record. No order is implied by a BUY/SELL condition.
4. A signal based on finalized Monday data **cannot fill at Monday's close**. Earliest calendar eligibility is Tuesday's regular session (or the next open session after a holiday/closure), only after actual signal availability and approval. If evidence/approval arrives after that session ends, use the next eligible open session; do not backdate. Friday normally advances to Monday, subject to the same calendar. Use America/New_York, not a fixed Dublin/UTC offset across DST differences.
5. Defensible future fill basis: an independently timestamped, actually available subsequent-session executable-price proxy, with documented feed/bid-ask or trade limitations, explicit adverse slippage and fees, and contemporaneous EUR_PER_USD evidence. Record it as a simulated proxy, never a real fill. An IEX last trade alone does not guarantee available liquidity. Operator approves quantity, stop, costs, FX and input/preview identity; recheck current portfolio and controls atomically before any internal simulated fill.
6. **Daily OHLCV alone cannot supply that forward fill.** Tuesday's open in a daily bar acquired after Tuesday's close was unavailable at Tuesday's opening decision. It may support a separately approved retrospective next-open benchmark with orders fixed earlier, but not a contemporaneous fill or a backdated workflow authorization. The present workflow requires price availability by preview and price time equal to execution time within a later open session; fabricating those timestamps would bypass causality. Daily highs/lows also cannot establish intraday event ordering, spread, liquidity, stop execution, gaps or partial fills. A timestamped intraday price source and reviewed interface would be needed for the proposed forward proxy; no such request or implementation is authorized here.
7. After an authorized simulation, reconcile the existing ledger, inspect rejections and controls, and retain only permitted records. Never auto-clear kill/latch/quarantine. No catch-up fills after operator absence. At day 90 stop new entries and apply the pre-agreed valuation/position disposition policy without inventing liquidation fills. Dividends, splits and FX costs must be supported or the resulting performance must remain incomplete; the broker has no automatic corporate-action accounting.
