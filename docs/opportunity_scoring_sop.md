# Opportunity Scoring SOP

## Purpose

This document defines the uniform method used by Llama Capital
to score event-driven investment opportunities.

Agents must score each parameter separately.
Agents must not select the final score directly.
The final score is calculated from the weighted parameters.

## Scoring Parameters

| Parameter | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Event impact | 30% |
| Evidence reliability | 25% |
| Growth potential | 20% |
| Market underreaction | 15% |
| Risk quality | 10% |

## Parameter Scale

Each parameter is scored from 0 to 10.

- 0–2: Very weak
- 3–4: Weak
- 5–6: Moderate
- 7: Credible and meaningful
- 8: Strong and well-supported
- 9: Exceptional
- 10: Extraordinary; use only with overwhelming evidence

## Final Score Interpretation

- 0–3.9: Reject
- 4.0–5.9: Monitor
- 6.0–6.9: Preliminary research
- 7.0–7.9: Investment candidate
- 8.0–8.9: High-priority candidate
- 9.0–10.0: Exceptional opportunity requiring immediate review

## Score 7

A score of 7 means the opportunity is credible and meaningful,
but material uncertainty or unresolved risk remains.

## Score 8

A score of 8 means the opportunity is strong, supported by reliable
evidence, financially material, and potentially not fully priced in.

## Control Rules

- Every factual claim must include a source.
- Missing evidence reduces Evidence Reliability.
- Unresolved contradictions must be reported.
- A score of 9 or 10 requires QA and iQA review.
- The agent must explain every parameter score.
